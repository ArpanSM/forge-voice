# Installed skills

These skills are vendored for the demo-video build and keep their original licences.

- `argo-guide/` — Argo demo-video guide, from `@argo-video/cli` 0.42.1. Licence: MIT (see `argo-guide/LICENSE`).
- `video-director/` — Video Director, a film-direction and QA skill. Licence: MIT (see `video-director/LICENSE`).

Neither skill was modified. The demo script and video are original work in this repository.

- `huashu-motion/` — Huashu Motion, a launch-film and motion-graphics skill by Huashu. Licence: MIT (see `huashu-motion/LICENSE`).

The Huashu film in `huashu-film/` was made with that skill; its bundled GSAP and font licences are recorded in `huashu-film/vendor/` and `huashu-film/assets/fonts/`.
